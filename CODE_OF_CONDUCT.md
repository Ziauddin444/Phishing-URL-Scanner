# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and users. This project is dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Giving credit to others' work and ideas

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Spam or unsolicited commercial promotions
- Disruption of community discussions
- Trolling or deliberate antagonism
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

### Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document** the incident with details (dates, messages, context)
2. **Report** to project maintainers via email
3. **Avoid** engaging further with the person if it feels unsafe
4. **Seek support** if you need it

Reports should include:
- Description of the incident
- Who was involved
- When it happened
- Any relevant messages or context
- Impact on you or others

### Consequences

Unacceptable behavior will result in:

1. **First Offense**: Warning and discussion
2. **Second Offense**: Temporary suspension from participation
3. **Third Offense**: Permanent removal from community

## Guidelines

### For Contributors

✅ **Do:**
- Be respectful and constructive
- Ask for help when needed
- Acknowledge others' contributions
- Be open to feedback
- Assume good intent
- Keep discussions on-topic

❌ **Don't:**
- Make assumptions about others
- Use insults or put-downs
- Share confidential information
- Engage in off-topic debates
- Post spam or promotional content

### For Maintainers

✅ **Do:**
- Respond fairly and consistently
- Document decisions
- Give people chances to improve
- Handle reports confidentially
- Lead by example

❌ **Don't:**
- Show favoritism
- Ignore violations
- Make unilateral decisions
- Public shaming
- Retaliate against reporters

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, PRs)
- Project events and conferences
- Community interactions
- Official communications

This includes but is not limited to:
- GitHub issues and discussions
- Pull request comments
- Email communications
- Social media mentions
- Project events

## Escalation

If standard enforcement doesn't work:

1. **Escalate** to senior maintainers
2. **Document** all attempts at resolution
3. **Seek** external mediation if needed
4. **Final decision** by project leadership
